Task details:

Imagine you’re given an array of prices, and your job is to pick out only the expensive ones.

Here’s the array: prices = [ 15.99, 125.50, 8.75, 200.00, 35.20 ]

Your task:

Describe, in simple steps, how you would use a regular for loop (the kind that starts with i = 0) to go through each price in the list. As you loop, you want to collect only the prices that are greater than 100 and put them into a new array.

Hint:

Start by creating an empty array where you'll store the expensive items.
